WHAT ARE HEART POINTS?

❤️ Heart Points are Google Fit's way of measuring how much your activities benefit your heart and health.


🚶 1 Heart Point = 1 minute of moderate activity (brisk walking, light cycling)

🏃 2 Heart Points = 1 minute of vigorous activity (running, HIIT, swimming)


🏆 WHO Recommendation: Earn at least 150 Heart Points per week (~22/day) for major health benefits — reduced risk of heart disease, diabetes, and stroke.


⭐ Reaching 500 Heart Points/week provides even greater benefits according to research.
